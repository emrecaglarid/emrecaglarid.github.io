Emre Caglar

I help build B2B experiences for|humans and agents. Currently doing product design at n8n.

I'm a product designer at n8n, working on how people come to trust AI agents. That means making agent evaluations something anyone can run, not just ML engineers, and designing how people stay in control of agents that automate work across the tools they use.

Before n8n, I spent five years designing enterprise products at Supermetrics. I joined as the second designer and grew into a principal IC, leading and coaching designers on the company's most complex product surface and designing data workflows for marketers and analysts at scale.

My background is in design research. I completed a PhD on design fiction, exploring how narrative theory applies to world-building in real design processes, and continued at Aalto University as a visiting researcher.

Based in Helsinki.
